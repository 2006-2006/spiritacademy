## 🔧 TECHNICAL IMPLEMENTATION

### **Authentication Flow:**
```tsx

  ↓ (Click "Start Your Journey")

  ↓ (Email/Password or Google OAuth)

  ↓ (Complete 3-step form)

```

### **State Management:**

- ✅ Debounced mouse tracking
